CounterpartyTransactionsNet totalCategory

One rule per line: Category: pattern. The pattern is a case-insensitive regex on the counterparty name and description. Optional tokens: type:bc|ec (transaction type codes), in / out, foreign (has a foreign-currency amount). Lines starting with # are ignored.

Recurring payments

Direct debits, transfers and other non-card payments to the same counterparty, of a similar amount (±15%), about once a month. "Name" adds up all recurring amounts per counterparty name, across IBANs. Indicative: a short statement period only shows a few repeats. Click a row to filter the table.
